# Confidence Aggregation

Confidence aggregation combines candidates from rules, ontology and similarity. Registered ML, DL and LLM classifiers can contribute later after model registry and evaluation are in place.

Decision states:

- `CONFIRMED`
- `HIGH_CONFIDENCE`
- `POSSIBLE`
- `AMBIGUOUS`
- `CONFLICTING`
- `UNKNOWN`

Unclear or close competing candidates require review.
