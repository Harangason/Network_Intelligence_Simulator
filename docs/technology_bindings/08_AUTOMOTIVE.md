# Automotive / Vehicle

Registered families cover CAN 2.0, CAN-FD, CAN XL, LIN, FlexRay, MOST, CANopen, J1939, ISOBUS, UDS, XCP, CCP, SOME/IP, SOME/IP-SD, DoIP, OBD-II, AVB, and TSN.

CAN/CAN-FD, LIN, SOME/IP, and their required generic stack layers are executable. Remaining entries expose their exact partial, experimental, legacy, or planned status through the registry.
