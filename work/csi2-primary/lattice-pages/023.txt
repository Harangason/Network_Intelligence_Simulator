You can increase the available buffering margin by adjusting the Line Buffer Depth parameter in the IP configuration. A

When backpressure is asserted (axis_vid_tready_o = low), the upstream video source is expected to pause pixel 
transmission immediately until tready returns high. The upstream logic must be able to throttle or halt data flow on 
tready deassertion, otherwise, incoming data may be lost or misaligned.
