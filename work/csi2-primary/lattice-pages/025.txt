2.4.2.3. CRC Insertion of the MIPI CSI/DSI Transmitter 
CRC handling is controlled independently of ECC insertion. When CRC insertion is enabled, the transmitter recalculates 
the long-packet CRC based on the received payload and appends the updated CRC value before packet transmission. 
This allows payload data to be modified upstream while ensuring packet integrity at the CSI-2 protocol level. When CRC 
insertion is enabled, the CRC field must still be present in the incoming packet, but the value is treated as don’t-care 
and is overwritten by the transmitter. When CRC insertion is disabled, the transmitter forwards the packet payload and 
CRC field unmodified, preserving the original packet contents end-to-end. The upstream logic must provide a valid CRC, 
as the field is forwarded unchanged.

asserted during a TX transaction for 32-bit passthrough mode in Figure 2.20 and 64-bit passthrough mode in Figure 
2.21.
