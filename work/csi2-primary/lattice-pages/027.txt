When MIPI Byte Streaming Interface is enabled, a single clock FIFO is used to hold data when downstream is not ready 
(TREADY is low). FIFO depth is configurable through the Byte Buffer Depth attribute. If TREADY remains low for a 
duration greater than or equal to the configured buffer depth, the buffer can overflow, leading to functional errors.
