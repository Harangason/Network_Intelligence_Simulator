To achieve a desirable target frame rate, the transmitter is required to dynamically insert horizontal and/or vertical 
blanking periods to regulate the frame period duration of the video frame output. With additional blanking insertion, 
the transmitter introduces additional duration at LP-11 state to throttle the serial streaming output and maintain the 
frame rate to be constant. 
As the number of lanes increases with fixed lane rate, the active pixel data is distributed across all lanes thus active 
data transmission time is reduced. To maintain the same frame rate across different number of lanes, the required 
horizontal line or vertical frame blanking period duration at LP-11 (Stop/Idle) state needs to be adjusted accordingly. 
The following table describes an example of expected frame rate derived with no overhead: 
• Active Resolution: 1920 × 1080 
• Horizontal Blanking Pixels: 280 
• Vertical Blanking Lines: 45 
• Total Horizontal Pixels: 2,200 
• Total Vertical Lines: 1,125 
• Pixel format: RAW10 
• Lane rate: 912 Mbps 
• Pixels Per Clock: 4 
• Byte Clock (Lane rate/PHY Data Width): 114 MHz
