Programming T_SKEW_INITIAL with a value of 65 
(0x041) sets a skew calibration period of 65 × 6.4 ns. 
When triggered via the CAL_PERIODIC_EN register bit, 
this results in a calibration time of approximately 
416.0 ns across the serial links.
