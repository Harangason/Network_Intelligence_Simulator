9.3. Horizontal and Vertical Blanking Period Insertion Tuning 
As the overheads vary with different PHY configuration settings in TX, Lattice recommends simulating and analyzing 
using the IP simulation to determine the accurate horizontal and vertical blanking period to be inserted at TX to achieve 
the desired frame rate for specific resolution. 
The general formula for Frame Period and Line Period are as follows: 
Frame Period = 1/Frame Rate Per Second 
Line Period = Line Start Short Packet HS burst duration (Optional) + LP-11 minimum duration + Long Packet HS burst 
duration + LP-11 minimum duration + Line End Short Packet HS burst duration (Optional) + Horizontal line blanking 
duration 
Where: 
• SOT duration is the summation of TLPX, T_HS-PREPARE, and T_HS-ZERO time which is required for LP to HS 
transition. 
• EOT duration is the T_HS_TRAIL time which is required for HS to LP transition.  
• LP-11 minimum duration is the minimum LP-11 state duration imposed in TX from Line Start Short Packet HS burst 
to Long Packet HS burst or from Long Packet HS burst to Line End Short Packet HS burst. 
• The Line Start and Line End Short Packet HS bursts are optional packets depending on the TX configuration.
