7.4.7.2 Channel Requirements
For optimal AEQ performance and error free operation, the end-to-end transmission channel (Including 
cables, connectors, and PCBs) needs to meet insertion loss, return loss (impedance control), and crosstalk 
requirements given in Table 7-6 and Table 7-7. Poor impedance control or insertion loss of the transmission 
channel and poor channel to channel isolation (low IL / FEXT) may result in significant reductions in the 
maximum transmission distance.
