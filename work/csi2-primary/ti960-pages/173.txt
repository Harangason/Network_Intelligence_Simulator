8.4 Power Supply Recommendations
This device has separate power and ground pins for different portions of the circuit. This is done to isolate 
switching noise effects between different sections of the circuit. Separate planes on the PCB are typically not 
required. The Section 5 section provides guidance on which circuit blocks are connected to which power pin 
pairs. In some cases, an external filter many be used to provide clean power to sensitive circuits such as PLLs.
8.4.1 VDD Power Supply
Each VDD power supply pin must have a 10-nF (or 100-nF) capacitor to ground connected as close as possible 
to DS90UB960-Q1 device. TI recommends having additional decoupling capacitors (1 µF and 10 µF) and the 
pins connected to a solid power plane.
