CSI-2 Connector
Route CSI traces on 1 
or 2 inner signal 
layers each 
sandwiched with GND 
or power planes to 
form coupled 
striplines
Ensure pair-pair gap 
is > 5W* for minimal 
pair-pair coupling

Route CSI traces as 
100-/g1 differential 
coupled striplines 
(S=2W*) with tight 
impedance control 
(±10%)
Bring CSI traces to 
the inner layers close 
to the CSI pins
*W is a trace width.  S is a 
gap between adjacent 
traces.
Ensure CSI traces are 
length-matched to 
minimize intra-pair and 
inter-pair skew 
(recommended   5 mils)  
Avoid acute angles 
when routing CSI 
traces
