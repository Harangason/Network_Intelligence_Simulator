8.3 Cipher suite selection 275 
 276 
The security strength of the resulting TLS session is dependent on the cipher suite negotiated 277 
between the TLS end points. Cipher suites designate what cryptography will be used by the TLS 278 
session to provide a certain level of security.  279 
 280 
Only cipher suites registered with IANA and not known to have current weaknesses should be 281 
used in mbaps. 282
