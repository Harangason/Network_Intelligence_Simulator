3.3.3.2 Compatibility  between 4-Wire and 2-Wire cabling 
In order to connect devices implementing a 2-Wire physical interface to an already existing 4-Wire system, the 4-Wire cabling s ystem 
can be modified as described below :  
/square4 TxD0 signal shall be wired with the RxD0 signal, turning them to the D0 signal 
/square4 TxD1 signal shall be wired with the RxD1 signal, turning them to the D1 signal. 
/square4 Pull-up, Pull-down and line terminations resistors shall be re-arranged to correctly adapt the D0, D1 signals.
