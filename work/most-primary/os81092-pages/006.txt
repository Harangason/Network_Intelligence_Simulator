2.0 OS81092 PINOUT
Input pins must not be left floating; therefore, they must be driven, have pull-ups or pull-downs, or be tied directly to a
power or ground pin.
Digital pins that can be configured as outputs (e.g. DI/O, DOUT, DI/OD) are high impedance during power-up/reset. After
power-up/reset:
• pins with more than one configuration option are as follows:
- Pin 27 - DSCL functionality (DI/OD) - assumes TMS high
- Pin 31 - DSDA functionality (DI/OD) - assumes TMS high
- Pin 34 - MLBSIG functionality (DI/O) - default can be changed via the INIC Configuration String
- Pin 35 - MLBDAT functionality (DI/O) - default can be changed via the INIC Configuration String
- Pin 37 - High impedance until configured
- Pin 38 - High impedance until configured
- Pin 45 - ERR functionality (DOUT) - unless held low at the rising edge of reset
- Pin 46 - DINT functionality (DOUTD)
• output pins with a disable option remain in the high impedance state, unless specifically enabled by software (e.g. 
RMCK, MLBCLK), and
• all other pin types are as shown in Table 2-1.
