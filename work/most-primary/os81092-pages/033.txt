8.3 MediaLB Commands and Responses Not Supported
The OS81092 INIC does not support the following MediaLB Commands and RxStatus responses:
• Normal Commands:
- All Isochronous Commands
• System Commands:
- E0h - MOSTLock
- E2h - MOSTUnlock
- E4h - MLBScan
- E6h - MLBSubCmd
-F E h  -  MLBReset
• System Responses:
- 80h - DevicePresent
- 82h - DeviceServiceRequest
