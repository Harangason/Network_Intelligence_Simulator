15.6 ERXP/ERXN - Eye Mask Pattern
The OS81092 ERXP/ERXN receiver will tolerate an in put signal that runs up to  the keep-out boundaries of
the eye mask pattern in Figure 15-4. A differential signal received at ERXP/ERXN that fills the eye mask (up
to the keep-out boundary) represents the worst-case signal that is tolerable by the OS81092 receiver.
