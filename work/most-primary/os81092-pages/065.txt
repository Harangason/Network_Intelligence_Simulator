16.0 APPLICATION INFORMATION
The OS81092 Evaluation Board provides a platform for co mprehensive understanding of a MOST50 Network device
and implements various application concepts discussed in this chapter. Refer to OS81092 Evaluation Board User’s
Guide [11] for more information.
16.1 Power
The OS81092 requires 3.3 V power for the VDDA33, VDDEn, and VDDPn pins, and 1.8 V power for the VDDA18 and
VDDCn pins. In addition, for INIC to maintain critical network parameters while in a low power mode, a continuous 3.3 V
power supply should be connected to VDDU to power a small block of persistent memory. See Section 16.5 “Persistent
Memory” for more information.
The voltage on VDDA18 and VDDCn must never be more than 0.5 V above the VDDA33, VDDEn, VDDPn, and VDDU
pins. Furthermore, digital input pins must never be driv en to more than the VDDPn supply. These requirements are
applicable to power-up and power-down, as well as normal operating conditions.
Figure 16-1 illustrates a typical power supply and decoupling arch itecture for the OS81092 required to minimize jitter

ware Concepts Specification [8]. Although Figure 16-1 illustrates connections to other parts, only logical connections are
shown and the respective data sheet should be consulted fo r complete information on external components (such as
proper decoupling).
