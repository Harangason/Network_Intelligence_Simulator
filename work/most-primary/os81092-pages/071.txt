16.8 Physical Layer
Capacitors in the signal path should be C0G type for accuracy and stability. These capacitors include: C15, C16, C17,
C18, and C19. The C22, C23, C24 and C25 capacitors may be C0G type, as C0G is more commonly available for this
value. Additionally, these capacitors are AC grounds for the transformer center taps, thus should be rated for 50 V.

components are generic and may be obtained from various suppliers.
