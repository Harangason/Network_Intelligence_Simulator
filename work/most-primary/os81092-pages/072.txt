16.9 Layout Guidelines
INIC board designs should follow the basic guidelines outlined throughout this chapter, with special consideration given
to component selection and placement. Figure 16-4 shows an OS81092 pin subset including the typical external com-
ponents required for power supply decoupling.

ground and power plane layers are also provided. Specific layout recommendations are listed in the subsections that
follow.
