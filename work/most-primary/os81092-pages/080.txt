16.9.3 MediaLB INTERFACES
The OS81092 Media Local Bus  (MediaLB) 3-pin and 5-pin interfaces ar e provided to facilitate high-speed data
exchange between INIC and the EHC. MediaLB is intended for use on a single PCB. For effective operation, especially
at higher bus speeds, signal routing and component placement is extremely important.
16.9.4 THERMAL CONSIDERATIONS
The paddle of the OS81092 QFN package should be soldered to the thermal land (thermal pad) for efficient heat dissi-
pation. The thermal pad should be at least as large as the exposed portion of the package paddle and be connected to
the ground plane of the board using heat tubes (vias without thermal reliefs). At least nine heat tubes are recommended,
with a drill hole size no larger than 0.33 mm (13 mils).

16.9.5 MISCELLANEOUS
Floating areas of copper fill near INIC should be avoided du e to the risk of noise coupling into the system. A keep-out
can be used around the OS81092 and its immediate peripheral circuitry (e.g. decoupling capacitors and external crystal)
to ensure islands are not created when copper fill is poured. If a keep-out is not used, multiple stitching vias should tie
any floating copper fills to an appropriate reference on other power or ground layers.
The XTI and XTO traces should be equal in length. Additionally, t he distance between INIC and the external crystal
should be minimized.
