Application  
Field of use:   Bus cable for the Multifunction Vehicle Bus (MVB) for serial data communication in railway vehicles. 
   MVB is a component of the Train Communication Network (TCN) and standardized in IEC 61375-3-1. 
Performance:   Screened foiled star quad cable, having a nominal impedance of 120 Ω. Designed for transmission rates of 
   1.5 Mbit/s. The MVB transmits time-critical control signals in real time. 
Characteristics:  flame retardant, no flame propagation, halogen free, low smoke density, ozone resistant, UV resistant, 
   oil resistant, fuel resistant, resistant to acids and alkalis 
Applications:  MVB, TCN, RS-485 and others
