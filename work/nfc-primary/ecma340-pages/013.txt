9.2 Bit duration 
One etu equals 128/(D × fc), where the values of the divisor D depend on the bit rate and communication mode, 
see Table 1. 
Table 1 — Divisor D 
Communication mode bit rate Divisor D 
Active or Passive fc/128 (~106 kbit/s) 1 
Active or Passive fc/64 (~212 kbit/s) 2 
Active or Passive fc/32 (~424 kbit/s) 4 
Active fc/16 (~848 kbit/s) 8 
Active fc/8 (~1 695 kbit/s) 16 
Active fc/4 (~3 390 kbit/s) 32 
Active fc/2 (~6 780 kbit/s) 64 
 
NOTE 1 The Initiator selects the communication mode (either Active or Passive) and bit rate ( fc/128, fc/64 or fc/32 
specified by the following clauses). 
NOTE 2 This document does not specify the modulation and the bit coding beyond the bit rate of fc/32. 
9.3 Active communication mode 
9.3.1 General 
Targets and Initiators shall conform with the following specifications for both communication directions, i.e. 
Initiator to Target and Target to Initiator. 
9.3.2 Requirements for fc/128 
9.3.2.1 Bit rate 
The bit rate for the transmission during initialisation and SDD shall be fc/128. 
9.3.2.2 Modulation 
The modulation shall be in accordance with ISO/IEC  14443-2:2020, 8.1.2 for a bit rate of fc/128. During 
transmission, both the Initiator and the Target shall conform to PCD values. During reception, both the Initiator 
and the Target shall conform to PICC values. 
9.3.2.3 Bit representation and coding 
The bit representation and coding shall be in accordance with ISO/IEC  14443-2:2020, 8.1.3 for a bit rate of 
fc/128. 
9.3.2.4 Byte transmission 
Initiators and targets shall transmit bytes with the lsb first.
