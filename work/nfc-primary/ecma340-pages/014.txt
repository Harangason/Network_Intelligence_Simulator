9.3.3 Requirements for fc/64 and fc/32 
9.3.3.1 Bit rates 
The bit rates for the transmission during initialisation and SDD shall respectively be fc/64 or fc/32. 
9.3.3.2 Modulation 
The modulation shall be in accordance with ISO/IEC  14443-2:2020, 9.1.2 for the bit rate of fc/64 and fc/32. 
During transmission, both the Initiator and the Target shall apply the PCD values. During reception, both the 
Initiator and the Target shall apply the PICC values. 
The Target should accept a modulation index range from 8  % to 30  % to operate with Initiators using a 
modulation index higher than 14 % for backward compatibility. 
9.3.3.3 Bit representation and coding 
Manchester bit encoding shall be employed as illustrated in Figure 1 and Figure 2. 
Bit coding format is Manchester with logic levels defined as: 
− Logic “ZERO”: The first half of the bit duration is carrier low field amplitude, and the second half of the bit 
duration shall be carrier high field amplitude (no modulation applied).  
− Logic “ONE”: The first half of the bit duration is carrier high field amplitude (no modulation applied), and the 
second half of the bit duration shall be carrier low field amplitude.  
Reverse polarity in amplitude shall be permitted. Polarity shall be detected from the SYNC.

9.3.3.4 Byte transmission 
Initiators and Targets shall transmit bytes with the msb first.
