9.4 Passive communication mode 
9.4.1 Requirements for fc/128 
9.4.1.1 Initiator to Target requirements 
See 9.3.2. 
9.4.1.2 Target to Initiator requirements 
9.4.1.2.1 Bit rate 
See 9.3.2.1. 
9.4.1.2.2 Modulation 
The modulation shall be in accordance with ISO/IEC 14443-2:2020, 8.2.2. 
9.4.1.2.3 Subcarrier Frequency 
The subcarrier frequency shall be in accordance with ISO/IEC 14443-2:2020, 8.2.3 for a bit rate of fc/128. 
9.4.1.2.4 Subcarrier modulation 
The subcarrier modulation shall be in accordance with ISO/IEC 14443-2:2020, 8.2.4 for a bit rate of fc/128. 
9.4.1.2.5 Bit representation and coding 
The bit representation and coding shall be in accordance with ISO/IEC  14443-2:2020, 8.2.6 for a bit rate of 
fc/128. 
9.4.1.2.6 Byte transmission 
Initiators and Targets shall transmit bytes with the lsb first.

9.4.2.1.2 Modulation 
The modulation shall be in accordance with ISO/IEC  14443-2:2020, 9.1.2 for the bit rate of fc/64 and fc/32. 
During transmission, the Initiator shall apply the PCD values.
