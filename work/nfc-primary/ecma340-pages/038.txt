12.6.3.2.2 Definition of the PSL_RES bytes 
CMD1: Shall be set to ‘D5’. 
CMD2: PSL_RES 
The PSL_RES byte shall specify the command Parameter Selection response for the Target device. The value 
of PSL_RES shall be ‘05’. 
Byte 1: DID 
The DID shall be the same as the DID defined during ATR or WUP.
