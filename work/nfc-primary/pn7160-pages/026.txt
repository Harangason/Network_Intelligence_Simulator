11.3.2  Integrated PLL to make use of external clock
When enabled, the PLL is designed to generate a low noise 27.12 MHz from an input clock 19.2 MHz, 26 MHz,
32 MHz, 38.4 MHz and 48 MHz.
The 27.12 MHz of the PLL is used as the time reference for the RF front end.
