11.4.2  DSLDO: Dual Supply LDO
The input pin of the DSLDO regulator is VBAT.
The output of this regulator (VDD) is internally connected to supply the internal digital blocks which are on VDDD.
It must be externally de-coupled and VDDD must be connected to VDDA.
11.4.3  TXLDO
Transmitter voltage is generated by internal LDO (VDD(TX)).
This TXLDO allows a maximum continuous current load up to 250 mA in order to support ISO/IEC 14443 and
NFC Forum standard compliant operations.
The Low Drop Out regulator has been designed to reject the noise which could interfere with the RF
communication.
