11.4.3.3  TXLDO limiter
The TXLDO includes a current limiter to avoid too high current within TX1 and TX2.
The current limiter block compares an image of the TXLDO output current to a reference, when the reference is
reached the output current gets limited.
The limiting current is 300 mA ± 30 mA, above the specified maximum current allowed for RF operation (250
mA).

11.4.4  Very low-power RF field detector
A very specific use case is the RF field detection when the NFCC is in Power Off mode. In this scenario, the
NFCC should detect the presence of an external magnetic field and notify the host system about its presence.
