11.6.1.3  R/W mode for NFC Forum type 4 Tag (T4T) type B
The R/W mode for the NFC Forum Type 4 Tag of type B is the general reader to card communication scheme
according to the ISO/IEC 14443B specification. Figure 28 describes the communication on a physical level, the
communication table describes the physical parameters.
