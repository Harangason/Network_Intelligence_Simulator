11.6.1.4  R/W mode for NFC Forum Type 5 Tag
The R/W mode for NFC Forum Type 5 Tag (T5T) is the general reader to card communication scheme
according to the ISO/IEC 15693 specification. PN7160 communicates with VICC (Type 5 Tag) using only 26.48
kbit/s with single subcarrier.
PN7160 supports the commands as defined by the ETSI HCI (see ref.[12]) and on top offers the inventory of the
tags (anti-collision sequence) on its own.
