11.6.4  Frequency interoperability
When in communication, PN7160 is generating some RF frequencies. PN7160 is also sensitive to some RF
signals as it is looking from data in the field.
In order to avoid interference with others RF communication, it is required to tune the antenna and design the
board according to ref.[7].
Although ISO/IEC 14443 and ISO/IEC 18092/Ecma 340 allows an RF frequency of 13.56 MHz ± 7 kHz, FCC
regulation does not allow this wide spread and limits the dispersion to ± 100 ppm, which is in line with PN7160
capability, see Table 17 and Table 18.
