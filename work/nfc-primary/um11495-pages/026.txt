5   Compliance to [NCI] and PN7160 extensions
The PN7160 is a complex contactless System on Chip, which offers a lot of features. Unfortunately, NCI as
defined by the NFC Forum refers in this document to NCI 2.0 but does not give full access to all possible
features. Therefore, NXP had to extend NCI with proprietary extensions.
