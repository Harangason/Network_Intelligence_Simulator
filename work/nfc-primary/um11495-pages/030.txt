5.3.4  Compliance to [NCI] RF Discovery
[NCI] relies on the NFC Forum Activity Specification specification defined by the NFC Forum.
NFC Forum Activity Specification v1.1 is applied by default on PN7160.
Note:  In the following sections, [ACTIVITY] refers to the common parts of NFC Forum Activity Specification
v1.0 and NFC Forum Activity Specification v1.1.
