6.5  TVDD configurations
PN7160 supports 2 different TVDD configurations, called CFG1 and CFG2 (with or without external DC-DC).
See PMU_CFG in Section 11.1 for the configuration options.
6.5.1  CFG1: VDD(UP) connected to VBAT
In CFG 1, VDD(UP) and VBAT are connected to an external supply delivering between 2.8 V and 5.5 V.
Figure 36. CFG1 - VDD(UP) connected to VBAT
6.5.2  CFG2: VDD(UP) connected to external power supply
In CFG2, the VDD(UP) pin is connected to an external power supply. The internal TXLDO is used to generate
configurable TVDD.
Figure 37. CFG2 - VDD(UP) connected to external power supply
An external DC-DC can alternatively be used to supply VDD(UP).
Figure 38. CFG2 - VDD(UP) connected to external DC-DC
