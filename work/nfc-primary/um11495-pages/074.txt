8   Listen side: Card Emulation Mode
8.1  ISO-DEP based on NFC-A and NFC-B
For Card Emulation hosted by the DH based on either technology NFC-A or technology NFC-B, the PN7160
only supports the ISO-DEP protocol.
[NCI] defines all the mechanisms necessary to implement this feature. Two options are possible:
1. The DH wants to manage by itself the ISO-DEP protocol, it SHALL then map the ISO-DEP protocol on the
Frame RF Interface.
2. The DH leaves the ISO-DEP protocol management to the NFCC: it SHALL then map the ISO-DEP protocol
on the ISO-DEP interface.
