9.1.4  WTX notification
As already described in previous chapter, the PN7160 comes with a proprietary notification WTX which
indicates that peers are in phase of exchanging RTOX REQ/RESP (NFC DEP equivalent of WTX in ISO DEP)
for the configured period of time. The notification description in Section 7.3.4 also applies in Initiator mode
(Active or Passive).
