12.3  TEST_PRBS_CMD/RSP
This command is used to start PRBS infinite stream generation. Both PRBS generation done by the FW or by
a specific HW block can be used, depending on what fits the needs the best. A 511-bit pseudo-random test
sequence (PRBS9) or a 32 767-bit pseudo-random test sequence (PRBS15) can be selected.
