Mounting the PRO-MUX-1
 
DIN Rail Mounting
Install the DIN rail in the desired location. 
Fully retract the black securing latch in to the down position.
Attach the PRO-MUX-1 to the DIN rail as shown in the diagrams below. 
Ensure the PRO-MUX-1 sits flush against the DIN rail, then push the securing latch back in to position  
to hold the PRO-MUX-1 in place.
Bulkhead Mounting
Actisense recommends the PRO-MUX-1 is installed using the DIN rail supplied for the most secure 
installation.  However, the PRO-MUX-1 can be secured to a bulkhead using the two screw catches on the 
reverse of the PRO-MUX-1 shown in the diagram below.  
Two screws must be used 89.4mm apart and horizontally aligned.
