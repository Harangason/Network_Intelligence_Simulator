2.5.3 NMEA latitude and longitude format
According to the NMEA standard, latitude and longitude are output in the format degrees, minutes
and (decimal) fractions of minutes . To convert to degrees and fractions of degrees , or degrees,
minutes, seconds and fractions of seconds , the minutes and fractional minutes  parts need to be
converted. For example:
Format Latitude Longitude
Receiver output $GNRMC,014230.00,A,4722.80340,N,00831.68218,E,0.000,,120477,,,A,V*14
(d)ddmm.mmmmm 4722.80340 North 00831.68218 East
Degrees and minutes 47 degrees, 22.80340 minutes 8 degrees, 31.68218 minutes
Degrees 47.38005667 degrees 8.52803633 degrees
Degrees, minutes and seconds 47 degrees, 22 minutes, 48.2040 seconds 8 degrees, 31 minutes, 40.9308 seconds
2.5.4 NMEA GNSS, satellite, and signal numbering
See GNSS, satellite, and signal identiﬁers for details on how GNSS, satellites and signals are
numbered in the NMEA protocol.
NMEA deﬁnes satellite numbering systems for some, but not all GNSS. The exact behavior depends
on the conﬁgured NMEA protocol version and ("extended" or "strict") mode. See NMEA protocol
conﬁguration for details.
