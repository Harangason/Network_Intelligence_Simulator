3.13.2.3 BeiDou health assistance
Message UBX-MGA-BDS-HEALTH
BeiDou health assistance
Type Input
Comment This message allows the delivery of BeiDou health assistance from D1/D2 ephemeris to a receiver.
See section AssistNow online in the integration manual for details.
This message allows the delivery of health assistance data for all satellites with svId 1 to 30.
