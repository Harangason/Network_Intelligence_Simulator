3.15.14.1 Reset odometer
Message UBX-NAV-RESETODO
Reset odometer
Type Command
Comment This message resets the traveled distance computed by the odometer (see UBX-NAV-ODO).
UBX-ACK-ACK or UBX-ACK-NAK are returned to indicate success or failure.
