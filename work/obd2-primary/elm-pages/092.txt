v1.4
Features:
- Added Low Power (sleep) mode
- Added extended addressing mode for 
CAN protocols

14230 support

manually initiated

data storage

when searching)

CEA, CV 0000, FI, IB48, IGN, LP, PB,

0E, 12, 15, 19, 2C, 2E
