Message: CertificateSignedRequest
- certificateChain <Certificate generated from the received CSR from step 3 and signed by the V2G Root or SubCA certificate from
the configured V2G certificate chain>
NOTE: The Test System will validate the certificate, but if the following validation fail, the testcase will NOT FAIL, because generating
the certificate is probably not be done by the CSMS.
- The key must be at least 224 bits long.
- The received certificate must be transmitted in the X.509 format encoded in Privacy-Enhanced Mail (PEM) format.
