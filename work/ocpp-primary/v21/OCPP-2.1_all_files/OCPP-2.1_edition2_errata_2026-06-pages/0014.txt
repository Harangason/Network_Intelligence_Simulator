2.10. Page 140 - (2026-06) - C22.FR.01/02 - clarify "without receipt
information" [1286]
Requirements C22.FR.01/02 mention that when settlement fails or is rejected, that Charging Station sends a
NotifySettlementRequest without receipt information. C21.FR.03, however, always requires a receiptUrl, either from Charging
Station or CSMS.
The intention of C22.FR.01/02 is that the receipt is not a real receipt because there was no settlement. It is, however, allowed to
provide a receiptUrl that points to a page that tells that the settlement has failed of was rejected. This is clarified by the following
change of wording.
