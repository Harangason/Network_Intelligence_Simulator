ID Precondition Requirement definition
F05.FR.03 F05.FR.01 AND
Specified connector unknown.
The Charging Station SHALL use the status:
UnknownConnector in the UnlockConnectorResponse.
F05.FR.04 F05.FR.01 AND
The Charging Station was able to unlock the
specified connector.
The Charging Station SHALL use the status: Unlocked in the
UnlockConnectorResponse.
F05.FR.05 F05.FR.01 AND
The Charging Station was NOT able to unlock
the specified connector.
The Charging Station SHALL use the status: UnlockFailed in
the UnlockConnectorResponse.
F05.FR.06 F05.FR.01 AND
No cable is connected to the connector.
The Charging Station SHALL attempt to unlock the connector,
even if no cable is detected and SHALL return the result of the
unlock attempt.
F05.FR.07 F05.FR.01 AND
The connector has no lock or a manual lock
(e.g. sType1)
The Charging Station SHOULD respond with RPC Framework
CALLERROR: NotSupported.
