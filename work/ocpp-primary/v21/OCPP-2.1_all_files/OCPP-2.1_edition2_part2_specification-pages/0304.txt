I08.FR.06 After a transaction has ended, that has an
associated driver-specific tariff in
transactionInfo.tariffId AND
This driver-specific tariff is no longer in use at
Charging Station
Charging Station SHALL remove the
associated tariff with tariffId =
transactionInfo.tariffId.
If the tariff is also in
use at another EVSE it
cannot be removed.
I08.FR.07 A driver-specific tariff is considered to be "in
use" at a Charging Station after an
AuthorizeResponse with a tariff has been
received, and until an EVConnectionTimeout
occurs or the transaction that is started for the
idToken has finished.
I08.FR.08 CSMS SHALL ensure that TariffTypes with a
different content have a different tariffId, such
that the tariffIds in CostDetailsType records
from historical transactions remain valid.
See use case I12
about reporting cost
details at end of
transaction.
Choice of tariffId is up
to CSMS. It can, for
example, be a
sequential number, a
UUID or a hash value
of tariff contents.
I08.FR.09 I08.FR.01 CSMS SHALL NOT provide the field validFrom
in a TariffType in AuthorizeResponse.
Driver-specific tariffs
have no validFrom
date.
I08.FR.10 I08.FR.04 AND
TariffType in AuthorizeResponse has a value
for validFrom
ChargingStation SHALL ignore the value of
validFrom.
Tariff acceptance
I08.FR.20 I08.FR.04 AND
EV Driver does not accept the tariff AND
Charging Station has already started a
transaction
Charging Station SHALL deauthorize the
idToken of the transaction and send a
TransactionEventRequest with triggerReason =
"TariffNotAccepted".
There will be no tariffId
and no cost
associated with
transaction. Without
authorization, no
energy will be
delivered. If
TxStopPoint contains
"Authorized" or
"PowerPathClosed"
this situation will end
the transaction with
stoppedReason =
DeAuthorized. 
(Same as I07.FR.30)
I08.FR.21 08.FR.04 AND
EV Driver does not accept the tariff AND
Charging Station has not yet started a
transaction
Charging Station SHALL deauthorize the
idToken of EV Driver.
A transaction can still
be started, for
example, with
TxStartPoint =
"EVConnected" upon
cable plug-in, but
without authorization,
no energy will be
delivered.
(Same as I07.FR.31)
I08.FR.22 I08.FR.04 AND
EV Driver has implicitly or explicitly accepted
the tariff
Charging Station SHALL associate the tariff
with the transaction by including
transactionInfo.tariffId once in the next
TransactionEventRequest message.
Implicit acceptance of
tariff can, for example,
be plugging cable into
EV.
(Same as I07.FR.32)
