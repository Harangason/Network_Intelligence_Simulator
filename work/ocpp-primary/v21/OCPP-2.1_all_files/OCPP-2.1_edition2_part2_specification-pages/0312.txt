ID. Precondition Requirements Notes
I12.FR.02 I12.FR.01 AND
TariffCostCtrlr.Enabled[RunningCost] =
true
Charging Station SHALL provide a
costDetails field of type CostDetailsType
without a chargingPeriods field in
TransactionEventRequest with eventType =
Started and every
TariffCostCtrlr.Interval[Cost] seconds
during the transaction for eventType =
Updated.
Providing running cost updates
needs to be enabled via
TariffCostCtrlr.Enabled[Running
Cost]. See
RunningCostEnabled and
CostInterval.
I12.FR.03 I12.FR.02 AND
If a TransactionEventRequest is sent only
to report a running cost update
The TransactionEventRequest SHALL
contain a triggerReason = RunningCost.
A running cost update is sent
every CostInterval seconds,
which can be part of a
TransactionEventRequest that
is sent for another
triggerReason.
I12.FR.04 I12.FR.01 Each chargingPeriods element in
costDetails SHALL contain a reference in
the field tariffId to the tariff that was used.
I12.FR.05 When costs are calculated from a
TariffFixedPriceType
The total of these costs SHALL be in
totalCost.fixed element
I12.FR.06 If the transaction was authorized by an
idToken or groupIdToken that was related
to a reservation, as defined in Introduction
Charging Station SHALL evaluate
TariffType.reservationTime and TariffType
.reservationFixed elements, once at start of
the transaction.
Reservation cost are applied
once at start of transaction. See
H03.FR.09.
I12.FR.07 I12.FR.06 AND
Costs are calculated from TariffType
.reservationTime
The total amount of reservation time,
calculated as the duration from the time
the ReserveNowRequest was received
until start of the transaction, SHALL be in
the totalUsage.reservationTime field and
total of these costs SHALL be in
totalCost.reservationTime element
This is a time-based reservation
cost
I12.FR.08 I12.FR.06 AND
Costs are calculated from a TariffType
.reservationFixed
The total of these costs SHALL be in
totalCost.reservationFixed element
This is a fixed reservation cost.
I12.FR.09 When costs are calculated from
TariffEnergyPriceType
The total amount of energy SHALL be in
the totalUsage.energy field and total of
these costs SHALL be in totalCost.energy
element
I12.FR.10 When costs are calculated from
TariffType.chargingTime
The total amount of charging time SHALL
be in the totalUsage.chargingTime field
and total of these costs SHALL be in
totalCost.chargingTime element
I12.FR.11 When costs are calculated from
TariffType.idleTime
The total amount of idle time SHALL be in
the totalUsage.idleTime field and total of
these costs SHALL be in totalCost.idleTime
element
I12.FR.12 PriceType SHALL have the cost excluding
tax in the exclTax field.
I12.FR.13 When a PriceType has one or more
associated TaxRateType
The inclTax field in PriceType SHALL be
calculated as exclTax * taxRates.tax for the
taxRates element with stack = 0 (or
absent), and then the taxRates element(s)
with stack = 1 is/are applied to the result
for stack = 0, and then the element(s)
taxRates with stack = 2 is/are applied to
the result for stack = 1, etc.
For example, an "energy tax"
(stack 0) is applied to the net
price, and a "VAT" (stack 1) is
applied on top of the net price
including "energy tax".
There can be multiple taxRates
elements with the same stack
value.
I12.FR.14 When Charging Station is not able to
calculate the CostDetailsType (partly or
entirely)
Charging Station SHALL set the field
failureToCalculate to true and optionally
add a failureReason with a human-
readable text about the reason. Charging
Station MAY include any values that it was
able to calculate.
