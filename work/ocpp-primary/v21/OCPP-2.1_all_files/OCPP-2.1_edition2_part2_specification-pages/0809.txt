2.18. DER Control related
2.18.1. DCDERCtrlr
New in OCPP 2.1
The DCDERCtrlr component holds DER capabilities of the DC inverter of the EVSE in the Charging Station. The component is located
at the EVSE level, since it represents the DER capabilities, also referred to as nameplate information, of the EVSE.
