TC_K_121_CS: Dynamic charging profiles from CSMS - Pull
Test case name Dynamic charging profiles from CSMS - Pull
Test case Id TC_K_121_CS
Use case Id(s) K28
Requirement(s) K28.FR.08, K28.FR.09, K28.FR.10
System under test Charging Station
Description To enable the CSMS to influence the charging power or current drawn from a specific EVSE or the entire
Charging Station over a period of time. The CSMS sends a SetChargingProfileRequest to the Charging
Station to influence the power or current drawn by EVs. The CSMS calculates a ChargingSchedule to stay
within certain limits, which MAY be imposed by any external system.
Purpose To verify if the Charging Station is able to support pulling DynamicControl charging profiles from the CSMS.
Prerequisite(s) SmartChargingCtrlr.SupportsFeature[DynamicProfiles] is true
