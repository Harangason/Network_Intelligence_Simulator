Tool validations
* Step 1:
Message: StatusNotificationRequest
- connectorStatus must be Available
Message: NotifyEventRequest
- eventData[0].trigger must be Delta
- eventData[0].actualValue must be Available
- eventData[0].component.name must be Connector
- eventData[0].variable.name must be AvailabilityState
* Step 3:
Message: TransactionEventRequest
- eventType must be Ended
- triggerReason must be EVCommunicationLost
- transactionInfo.chargingState must be Idle
- transactionInfo.stoppedReason must be EVDisconnected
- idToken.type Central OR NoAuthorization
IF idToken.type Central
- idToken.idToken <Configured BatterySwap_Idtoken>
ELSE IF idToken.type NoAuthorization
- idToken.idToken <Empty string>
END
* Step 5:
Message: BatterySwapRequest
- eventType must be BatteryOut
- requestId must be <Configured requestId>
- idToken.idToken must be <Configured valid_idtoken_idtoken>
For each inserted battery:
- batteryData.evseId must be <not omitted>
- batteryData.serialNumber must be <not omitted>
- batteryData.soC must be <not omitted>
- batteryData.soH must be <not omitted>
Post scenario validations:
State is EVDisconnectedBatterySwapping
