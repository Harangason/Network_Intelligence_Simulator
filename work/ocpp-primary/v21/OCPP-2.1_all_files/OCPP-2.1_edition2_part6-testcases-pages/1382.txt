chargingNeeds.requestedEnergyTransfer AC_single_phase

10000

10000
end if
2. The CSMS responds with a
NotifyEVChargingNeedsResponse.
4. The Test System responds with a
SetChargingProfileResponse with:
status Accepted
3. The CSMS sends a SetChargingProfileRequest

- If NotifyEVChargingNeedsResponseStatus was Processing, the

5. The Test System sends a NotifyEVChargingScheduleRequest
with
evseId <COnfigured evseId>
chargingSchedule <ChargingSchedule provided at step 3>
6. The CSMS responds with a
NotifyEVChargingScheduleResponse.

triggerReason <ChargingStateChanged>
transactionInfo.chargingState <Charging>
8. The CSMS responds with a TransactionEventResponse.
Tool validations
* Step 2:
Message: NotifyEVChargingNeedsResponse
- Status must be Accepted or Processing
* Step 3:
Message: SetChargingProfileRequest
- chargingProfilePurpose must be <TxProfile>
- transactionId must be <Provided transactionId from before>
* Step 4:
Message: NotifyEVChargingScheduleResponse
- status must be <Accepted>
