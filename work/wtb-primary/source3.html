TRAIN COMMUNICATION NETWORK - TCN

IEC 61375-1

TCN interconnects programmable equipment in rail vehicles for the support of: traction and vehicle control (remote control, doors, lights.....) remote diagnostics and maintenance passenger information. 

Two level hierarchy:

  • Higher level "WTB" - Wire Train Bus - Communication between Vehicles
  • Lower level "MVB" - Multifuctional Vehicle Bus - Communication in Vehicle

Detail information - project ROSIN.

Note: Except the standard mentioned above different communication protocols are used on lower level of hierarchy. For example in US LON protocol is proposed, CAN in different applications is used. 

Wire Train Bus - Basic Parameters:

Topology chain of cable sections forming a bus, each node is attached to two bus sections
Length 860m (22 vehicles), 32 nodes
Medium shielded twisted wire pair
Physical Redundancy duplicated physical medium
Signalling data rate 1,0 Mbit/sec
Isolation from the line galvanically by transformers
Options line fritting circuit for connector cleaning

Hardware
